ForgeShape

Check, repair, and convert 3D-print models on your Apple device — without a full CAD suite.

Quick answer

ForgeShape imports STEP, IGES, STL, OBJ, GLB, and 3MF models, checks them for what blocks printing, repairs what it can, and exports a qualified file. Everything runs on the device. It is not a slicer, not CNC CAM, and not a general CAD editor.
